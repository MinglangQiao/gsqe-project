Overview

3D Gaussian Splatting renders novel views of a reconstructed scene. Along a camera path, fine textures can appear soft and artifacts can vary from frame to frame.

This project investigates few-step video differential diffusion for enhancing sequences of 3DGS renders. The research question is whether a short denoising process can restore visual detail while keeping adjacent views consistent.

Method

The conceptual workflow treats consecutive renders along a camera path as a single clip for enhancement.

  1. 3DGS render sequence

    Render consecutive novel views along a camera path.

  2. Few-step differential diffusion

    Apply video diffusion to refine the render sequence.

  3. Enhanced render sequence

    Inspect frame detail and consistency as the viewpoint changes.
